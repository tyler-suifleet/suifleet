2. [Setup attempt](sf247-testnet-setup-attempt.json) and

   successful objects and the **same** 400,000,000 MIST ceiling, carrying forward
   every prior charge. Final parent spend/allowance: **281,276,815 MIST**.

**1,218,822,015 MIST (1.218822015 SUI)**, including failed transactions and all
