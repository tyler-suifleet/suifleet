signing-vector and parsed custody-type changes; the table distinguishes their
subsequent focused checks. No test failures were ignored. Initial local-chain
harness attempts exposed insufficient fixture gas, startup timing, shared-input
mutability and first-start replay assumptions; these were corrected. The chain
also exposed the daemon's nested-address type-string mismatch, fixed by exact
parsed struct-type comparison.

passed here. The implementation worker hands off after pushing; it does not
watch hosted checks or merge. Physical/mainnet and final production-provider /
manual real-wallet acceptance remain unrun in
