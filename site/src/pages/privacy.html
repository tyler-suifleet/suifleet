Privacy

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking pixels. The guide uses a small local script for stage navigation; it does not store your activity or send it anywhere. Pages, styles, images, scripts and videos are served from this site alone.

This website

  • Our static host necessarily receives your IP address and request details to serve a page, and may keep short-lived access logs for security and abuse prevention. We do not combine them with other data or use them for profiling.
  • If we ever add analytics, it will be off until you consent, and this notice will say so first.
  • If you email us, we keep the correspondence to reply and for our records. We do not sell or share it.

The console and public networks

  • The console at app.suifleet.io is a static web app. It talks directly from your browser to the Sui network, Walrus storage and, for private releases, Seal key servers. These providers receive the requests needed to serve your data. SuiFleet operates no server that receives your fleet data.
  • Management records are public. Device names, addresses, group membership, deployment targets and reports recorded on Sui can be read by anyone. Current records can be changed or retired, but historical transactions may remain available from network or archive providers. Retiring a device does not erase that history. Do not put personal or confidential data in device or group names.
  • Release artifacts and logs are encrypted in the browser or on the device before upload when you choose confidential delivery. Content you publish as public can be read by anyone.
  • Wallets, Google sign-in (through Mysten Labs' Enoki service) and network operators are independent services with their own privacy terms.
