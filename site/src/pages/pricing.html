Pricing

One price per device per month, with Sui and Walrus network fees included: SuiFleet funds each fleet's on-chain budget from the subscription, so you do not need to hold SUI or WAL.

Proposed prices. These are the proposed list prices from the business plan (2026-10-23), estimated from testnet measurements and current list prices. They are not yet a binding tariff: no mainnet bill has been measured and final terms are set in your order. The testnet preview is free.

Getting started

  • At cost, up to 100 devices: no SuiFleet margin, about $0.01 per device per month at default settings, billed annually. Community support.
  • Design partners: a small number of manufacturers can run up to 5,000 devices at cost for 12 months with managed onboarding, in exchange for feedback and a reference. Ask us.

Prices are fixed in USD for the term. If the cost of Sui operations rises sharply, renewals may be requoted with at least 30 days' notice; prices never change mid-term.
